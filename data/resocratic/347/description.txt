A grandmother knits blankets and sweaters for her community. A blanket requires 30 units of yarn and 5 hours of knitting. A sweater requires 20 units of yarn and 4 hours of knitting. The grandmother has 200 units of yarn available and 40 hours of knitting available. If the profit per blanket is $5.50 and the profit per sweater is $5, how many of each should she knit to maximize her profit?
