A company is planning to optimize its energy consumption by investing in three types of renewable energy sources: Solar, Wind, and Hydro. The company aims to balance cost efficiency with environmental impact. The company has a budget of $150,000 for energy investments. The company aims to produce at least 360 units of energy in total. The company wants to ensure that at least 100 units of energy come from each source. The cost of producing one unit of solar energy is $500, wind energy is $300, and hydro energy is $400. The environmental impact score for producing one unit of solar energy is 3, wind energy is 2, and hydro energy is 4. The company wants to minimize the Cost-Impact ratio of the investment. (The Cost-Impact ratio is defined as the total cost of energy production divided by the total environmental impact score.)
