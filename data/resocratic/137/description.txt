A tropical farmer has available 200 acres of land to grow pineapples and bananas. He likes bananas, but because they require so much more work, he can grow at most 4 times the amount of bananas as pineapples. In addition he must grow at least 40 acres of pineapples and at least 60 acres of bananas. If the profit per acre of pineapples is $200 and the profit per acres of bananas is $150, how many acre of each should he grow to maximize profit?
