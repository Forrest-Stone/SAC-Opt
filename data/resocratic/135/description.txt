Out of a stock of three electronic components A, B, and C given as 200, 120, and 150 respectively, it is required to produce two types of circuits. Circuit type 1 requires 20 resistors, 10 transistors, and 10 capacitors, while circuit type 2 requires 10 resistors, 20 transistors, and 30 capacitors. Circuit type 1 sells at Gh.5 cedis each and type 2 circuit at Gh.12 cedis each. Advise management about how many of each type should be made to get the maximum financial returns algebraically.

Table (Components required for each type of circuit):
| Component | Circuit Type 1 | Circuit Type 2 | Stock Available |
| --------- | -------------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| A (Resistors) | 20             | 10             | 200             |
| B (Transistors) | 10             | 20             | 120             |
| C (Capacitors) | 10             | 30             | 150             |
