A jewelry company produces and sells bracelets and rings. The bracelets are hand-crafted by a team who can make at most 4 bracelets per day. The rings are made by another team who can make at most 7 rings per day. All rings have to be approved by a master jeweler and he can check at most 30 jewels of either type per day. If the profit per bracelet is $700 and the profit per ring is $300, how many of each should the jewelry company make to maximize profit?
